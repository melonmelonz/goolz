Peer Review The Plan

Exercise 4 / 5 . Level 2 . 30 min

Goal: a second pair of eyes on the plan to catch the unrealistic estimate and the missing item I am too close to see. I swapped plans with a partner building a habit tracker. I read theirs against the five questions, they read mine, and the trade was honest in both directions -- the point is to find the hole before the sprint does, not to be nice.

The Five Questions I Ran On Their Plan
  1. Realistic for three days? Add up the hours. Any single task suspiciously large?
  2. Anything missing from Must Have? A broken feature they did not notice, a P1 they are stepping around?
  3. Anything in Must that is really a Should? A feature that is not essential for launch?
  4. Is Day 3 overloaded? It is deploy day -- four hours of Must work there means something has to move up.
  5. Are the tasks specific? "Fix bugs" is not a task. "Fix the 500 on POST /api/items" is.

What I told my partner

  • Missing Must: their habit tracker had "create a habit" in Must but "mark a habit done" sitting in Should -- and the whole app is marking habits done. A user who can create but never check off has a list, not a tracker. I told them: that is your one feature that proves it works, it belongs in Must.
  • Day 3 overloaded: they had the AI coaching message scheduled for Day 3 morning. New AI feature on deploy day. Move it to Day 2 afternoon or cut it to Should.
  • Vague task: "polish the UI" -- I made them turn it into "fix the streak counter resetting at midnight" and "align the habit cards on mobile," two specific things with a Done each.

What my partner told me

  • "Your 'documented user activity' is an M but it is really two things." Fair hit. Getting a user to use the live app is one task; capturing the screenshot and the feedback sentence is another, and the second one is the part people forget under pressure. I split it: "get user on live URL" (Day 2 afternoon) and "capture screenshots + one feedback line per user" (Day 3 afternoon) so the evidence does not get skipped.
  • "Block 2 on Day 1 is two S tasks back to back -- is that really under an hour?" Honestly, maybe not, because the edit-revalidation touches the data-fetching path and those always have a surprise. I left it in the slot but flagged that if it runs over, the input guard (the actual P1) wins and the revalidation slides to Day 2 -- P1 before P2, always.
  • "Is the empty state really a Must?" Same challenge as the verbal demo. It survives for the same reason: my one user opens an app with zero rows, so it is his first screen. But my partner made me write that justification down next to the item, so I can defend it at the gate instead of just asserting it.
The revision that mattered most

Splitting "documented user activity" into use-it and capture-it. Left as one M, the capture half was going to evaporate -- I would get my user on the live URL, feel good, and never take the screenshot, and then on Day 4 the gate asks for evidence and I have a warm memory instead of a PNG. Two tasks, two Definitions of Done, one of which is literally "the screenshot file exists." Cheap insurance against a requirement I was on track to half-do.

Deliverable

A revised plan with the partner's feedback folded in: the user-activity Must split into use-it and capture-it with separate Dones, an explicit P1-beats-P2 fallback noted on Day 1 Block 2, and the empty-state justification written down so it survives a challenge. Plan is realistic, specific, and Day 3 is still just bugfix and deploy. Time to execute Block 1.
