The Provider Swap

Exercise 9 / 9 . stretch . Level 3 . 30 min

Goal: option B -- swap the assistant route from OpenAI to Anthropic and feel what is OpenAI-specific versus what is just how LLM APIs work. There is also an obvious joke in pointing my hand-rolled route at the company whose model has been pair-programming this entire course with me, and I am not above it.

Everything else -- the route shape, the try/catch, the key hygiene, the frontend, the injected listings -- did not change by one character. That is the substrate the lecture promised: system instruction, user message, completion. Two SDK lines, three call-shape lines, one response-parsing line. Twenty minutes including reading docs, and most of that was the four-things scan of a second provider's documentation, which after this morning felt routine -- which I suppose was the day working as designed.

Trivia versus opinions

Response parsing (choices[0].message.content vs content[0].text) is trivia -- arbitrary shapes, memorize and move on. Two differences are actual design opinions. system as a top-level parameter rather than a message in the array makes the instruction/conversation boundary structural instead of conventional -- after Exercise 7, where my whole problem was things in the message pile claiming authority they should not have, an API that fences the system instruction off in its own field reads less like style and more like a stance. And mandatory max_tokens is the provider refusing to default my spending ceiling -- mildly annoying to type, hard to argue with, very Exercise-4-spending-limit in spirit.

The comparison paragraph, as assigned

The swap cost six lines and twenty minutes, which is the headline: the integration pattern is fully portable, and provider choice is a product decision -- pricing, model fit, rate limits -- not an architecture decision, provided the route boundary from Exercise 2 exists to contain the blast radius. Answer quality on my tiny board-assistant workload was equivalent with a different accent; the differences that will actually steer a future choice are the design opinions (structural system param, mandatory output ceiling) and the meta-observation that prompt injection wobbled both engines, because it is a property of the substrate, not the vendor. Kept OpenAI on main for course consistency; the Anthropic version lives on a branch, six lines away, which is exactly as far away as a fallback provider should be.
